Lungs and breathing

Breathlessness, a cough that lingers or an abnormal chest scan deserves a clear answer.

A pulmonologist looks at the whole picture: your history, an examination, breathing tests done in the clinic and any imaging you’ve had. The aim is a clear diagnosis first, then treatment you can live with.

Conditions we see

  • Asthma
  • COPD
  • Chronic cough
  • Unexplained breathlessness
  • Allergies and rhinitis
  • Interstitial lung disease
  • Bronchiectasis
  • Tuberculosis
  • Lung nodules
  • Abnormal chest X-ray or CT

Interventional pulmonology

Interventional pulmonology uses minimally invasive procedures, such as bronchoscopy, to look inside the airways, take samples and treat some conditions without open surgery. It is one of Dr. Gangi Reddy TK’s special interests. If a procedure is the right next step, it is explained and planned with you at the clinic.

Sleep medicine

Poor sleep is often a medical problem rather than a habit.

Obstructive sleep apnoea, where breathing repeatedly stops and starts during the night, is common, often missed and treatable. Left alone it raises blood pressure and makes diabetes and heart disease harder to control, which is why it sits naturally alongside the rest of this practice.

Consider a sleep assessment if

  • You snore loudly most nights
  • Someone has noticed you stop breathing or gasp in your sleep
  • You wake up unrefreshed, or with a headache
  • You doze off during the day, including at the wheel
  • You’ve struggled to fall or stay asleep for months

An overnight study in the clinic’s sleep lab shows what is happening while you sleep, and treatment is planned around the result.
