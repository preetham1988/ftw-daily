Tests and lab

The lab and testing rooms are part of the clinic. Your doctor orders the test, sees the result and goes through it with you, without sending you across the city for each step.

Book a test or health check

Blood investigations

For routine checks, diabetes, infections and monitoring long-term conditions.

Complete blood work, from a routine blood count to sugar, kidney, liver, thyroid and cholesterol tests.

Before the test

Some tests need you to fast for 8 to 12 hours beforehand. Plain water is fine. We’ll tell you when you book.

Pulmonary function tests

For asthma, COPD, chronic cough and breathlessness.

Measures how much air your lungs hold and how quickly you can breathe it out. Used to diagnose lung conditions and to see how well treatment is working.

Before the test

Don’t smoke or eat a heavy meal for a few hours beforehand, and wear loose clothing. Ask us whether to take your inhalers that morning.

Sleep lab

For loud snoring, sleep apnoea, daytime sleepiness and disturbed sleep.

An overnight study that records your breathing, oxygen level, heart rate and sleep while you sleep. It is the standard test for sleep apnoea.

Before the test

You’ll arrive in the evening and go home the next morning. Bring nightwear and your usual medicines, and avoid caffeine after midday and alcohol that evening.

Allergy tests

For asthma, rhinitis, hives and suspected food allergy.

Finds what you react to, such as dust mites, pollen, mould, pets or foods, so treatment and avoidance can be targeted.

Before the test

Antihistamines can affect the results. Ask us before you stop any medicine, and we’ll tell you how many days ahead to do it.

Eye evaluation

For routine checks, diabetes, cataract assessment and retinal problems.

Vision and glasses prescription, eye pressure, and a dilated examination of the retina by an eye specialist.

Before the test

Dilating drops blur your vision for a few hours. Bring sunglasses and arrange not to drive home. Bring your current glasses too.

Health checks

For a baseline, a regular review, or when something doesn’t feel right.

Three focused checks, each reviewed with a doctor afterwards.

  • CardiopulmonaryHeart and lung health, for breathlessness, chest symptoms, smokers and anyone with a family history.
  • DiabeticBlood sugar control and the organs diabetes affects most: the kidneys, eyes, nerves and heart.
  • HypertensiveBlood pressure and its effects on the heart, kidneys and the blood vessels of the eye.
