General medicine, practised by specialists.

AGR Medical Center is a doctor-led clinic in Bangalore for everyday illness and long-term conditions, with particular depth in the lungs, sleep and the eye. Consultation, tests and follow-up happen in one place.

Book an appointment Call +91 80 0000 0000

Most people end up choosing between a doctor who knows them and a specialist who knows the disease. AGR is set up so you don’t have to.

The clinic is run by two consultants. Dr. Gangi Reddy TK sees patients for general health, diabetes and blood pressure, and is the clinic’s lung and sleep specialist. Dr. Archana M runs the eye practice, from routine eye checks to cataract and retina surgery. Consultants from other departments visit the clinic as well.

Your doctors

Dr. Gangi Reddy TK

Pulmonologist and physician

Leads medical care at AGR: general health, diabetes, high blood pressure, lung disease and sleep disorders. Special interests in interventional pulmonology and sleep medicine.

The lab is part of the clinic.

Tests are done here, so results go straight back to the doctor who asked for them, and you talk them through in the same place.

Blood investigations
Full blood work, from a routine blood count to diabetes and cholesterol panels.
Pulmonary function tests
Breathing tests for asthma, COPD and unexplained breathlessness.
Sleep lab
Overnight studies for snoring, sleep apnoea and disturbed sleep.
Allergy tests
To find the triggers behind asthma, rhinitis and other allergies.
Eye evaluation
Vision, eye pressure and a dilated examination of the retina.
Health checks
Cardiopulmonary, diabetes and blood pressure checks.
